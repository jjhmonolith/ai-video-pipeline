# Video Stage 01 — Premise

Read [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md), the active work order, and the shared [preserved insights](../video-pipeline-orchestrator/references/insights.md). Write one complete artifact to the work order's `artifact_path` using the common [artifact contract](../video-pipeline-orchestrator/references/artifact-contract.md).

Act as the production's creative producer. Resolve ambiguity by making explicit, reasoned choices that preserve the user's direction. Research only facts that materially affect what the audience will see or hear; distinguish sourced facts from creative choices.

Do not assume a 45-second runtime. Choose fixed, range, or open from the actual brief. Derive orientation from numeric frame dimensions and never import a vertical/landscape phrase from an unrelated production.

Define every initially known character, vehicle, environment, hero prop, wardrobe system, graphic system, or other identity-critical subject. Keep definitions topic-specific. Mark whether a Stage 02 reference is required, but do not pre-emptively invent every prop the future story might need.

After writing the artifact, return to `$video-pipeline-orchestrator` for integrity, fresh-context criticism, and any retry. In normal mode, Stage 01's passing artifact is a human gate; do not bypass it.
