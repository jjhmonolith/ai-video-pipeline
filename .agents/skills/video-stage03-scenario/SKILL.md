# Video Stage 03 — Scenario

Read [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md), the Stage 01–02 receipts and artifacts, the work order, and the shared [preserved insights](../video-pipeline-orchestrator/references/insights.md).

Think like a screenwriter and production designer before thinking like a cinematographer. Build sequence and scene progression around place, time, point of view, dramatic purpose, events, and visible state change. Use estimated scene editorial ranges to challenge incident density, not to force mechanical beat arithmetic.

Invent a story-motivated prop, state, location detail, costume, interface, or interaction when it materially improves the scenario. Register every new visible production dependency as a `NEW-*` requirement with an explicit Stage 05 reference policy. Do not shrink the story to the subjects anticipated before the scenario existed.

Do not author shot IDs, cut IDs, lenses, camera moves, shot compositions, or exact shot durations. Stage 04 owns the translation from scenes to coverage and time.
