# Video Stage 04 — Shot design

Read [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md), all upstream receipts and their current artifacts, the active work order, and the shared [preserved insights](../video-pipeline-orchestrator/references/insights.md).

Work in actual production order: interpret scene intent, block subjects and action, design coverage, group shots into coherent setups, then choose individual shots. For every camera and temporal technique, state the dramatic reason. Stillness and movement are both valid; neither is a quota or global rule.

Author exact editorial duration for every timeline shot. Derive it from information, performance phases, camera travel, handles, and temporal technique. Describe subject, world, and camera time independently for slow motion, freeze time, orbit/bullet-time, time lapse, speed ramp, or other non-real-time designs.

Bind all visible cast and required reference subjects honestly. If two people are visible, use a two-person composition such as `two_shot` or `over_the_shoulder`; do not label it `single`. A host-centered passenger-shoulder image is an over-the-shoulder composition, not a single.

After a pass, return immediately to the orchestrator. Stage 04 has no standalone human gate before Stage 05.
